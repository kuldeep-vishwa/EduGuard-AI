What EduGuard AI Does

Everything you need to protect student success

Academic Health Score

A single composite score (0–100) calculated from attendance, assessments, assignments, GPA and faculty feedback — updated in real time.

IBM Granite AI Analysis

Generates plain-language intervention recommendations for each at-risk student using IBM watsonx.ai Granite models.

Attendance Tracking

Subject-wise daily attendance recording with automatic percentage calculations and below-threshold alerts.

Adaptive Assessments

Question bank with difficulty tiers. Faculty schedule assessments; students take them online. Results feed directly into risk calculations.

Smart Notifications

Role-tailored in-app notifications alert students, faculty, and administrators to risk changes, low attendance, and pending actions.

Faculty Messaging

Faculty can send direct messages or broadcast notices to their authorized students — delivered instantly as dashboard notifications.
